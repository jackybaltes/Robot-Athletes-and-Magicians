Moravec's Paradox

  • Assumption: Higher level (symbolic/conscious) reasoning is most important part of AI
    • Play chess, Go, ...
  • Truth: Hardest part is subconscious reasoning
    • Get up
  • Current state of AI (ML) uses deep learning, large language models. "Scale is all you need"

Robotics is Harder Than AI

  • Reality is messy. Inputs are uncertain, noisy, physical limits, hard real-time, ...
  • Getting large enough corpus of high quality training data is time consuming
  • Sim to real transfer is hard
  • You can ignore wrong output from your chatbot - wrong output of a robot is dangerous

Artificial Intelligence

  • Robustness and versatility are important aspects of intelligence
  • Humans are bad at solving problems optimally
  • Our robots can outperform humans in very specific tasks (machine vision, pick and place)
  • But they also walk into a wall three times in a row
  • AI is about never doing the stupid things
  • Minimum time of fully autonomous operation (Don't Mess Up Period - DMUP)
  • Paper and videos are patient
  • Robot competition provide crucial benchmarks for guiding research
  • Combining sports and robotics is a natural way to motivate teams and spectators
  • Federation of International Robot Sports Association (FIRA) http://www.firaworldcup.org presents several leagues for robot athletes
  • In 2024, we had approximately 1,100 participants at FIRA Robot WorldCup in Sao Luis, Brazil
